How Does BBRankings Work?

BBRanking's Psych Sheet scoring algorithm takes each swimmer's Top 4 scoring swims into consideration.

Swimmer names are grabbed from the varsity results pages on Swimrankings.

Their best times are then pulled from their individual athlete page, using results from the current season only.

For swimmers with equally-ranked swims, it will take their top-scoring swims with the highest World Aquatics points.

It then re-ranks all of the remaining swims (after scratching out non-Top 4 swims) to create the Psych Sheets.

This is a volunteer project: if you find any errors in the rankings / scoring, please reach out and I'll do my best to correct the algorithms!

Please direct any comments, critiques, or e-transfers to Yegor Semenyuk at yegorsemenyuk@gmail.com.
