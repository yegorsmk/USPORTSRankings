Notable Errors and Omissions for 2025

The UNB Reds were not ranked (my apologies - fixed now!), and other schools were underrated (McGill, Western, Laval, and Guelph).

The current system will not rank foreign nationals that have Swimrankings disabled, and only tags kids that have varsity team racing results.

That is, if swimmers show as being from France (which disables Swimrankings season's best functionality), their seasonal bests will be inaccurate.

If swimmers haven't competed at least once this year under the varsity tag (only competing as ROD instead of UOFR), they will not be grabbed.

This is a volunteer project: if you find any errors in the rankings / scoring, please reach out and I'll do my best to correct the algorithms!

Please direct any comments, critiques, or e-transfers to Baylee Munro at bayleemunro@gmail.com.
